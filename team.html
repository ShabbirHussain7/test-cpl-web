Team

Our research community is a vibrant and growing subfield that exists at the intersection of networking, security and privacy, Internet measurement, and social and political science. Its research problems are often broad and complex, with a potential to impact large populations of vulnerable users, and practitioners draw on (and advance) techniques from across these disciplines to address them. Censored Planet is founded and directed by Prof. Roya Ensafi.
